Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the SNESForge app for Android (package com.jonjackson.snesforge) and this website. SNESForge is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your games, saves, covers, controllers and settings are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • SNESForge goes online only when you ask for a box art cover (off until you switch it on) or tap Check for updates.

What stays on your phone

SNESForge keeps these in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Copies of the games you add, and their saves and save states.
  • Your library: titles, categories, collections, favourites and play time.
  • Cover pictures, controller designs and presets, and your settings.

Android's own backup is switched off for SNESForge. Backups are made only when you choose, as a ZIP file saved where you pick.

When SNESForge goes online

WhatWhere toWhat is sent
Box art (off until you switch it on)The public libretro-thumbnails picture collection on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com)Your IP address and the catalogue title of the game you asked a cover for. Never the game itself, your saves or anything else.
Check for updates (only when you tap it)GitHub (api.github.com)Your IP address and a request for the newest SNESForge version number.

Links in Settings, such as More apps and Website, open in your browser, which is covered by the browser's own policy. Android's file picker may show cloud storage apps you have installed; those apps handle their own transfers.

What the app can reach

AccessWhy
Files and folders you pickAdding games, covers and backups. SNESForge sees only what you choose in Android's file picker. It does not ask for access to all your files.
InternetBox art and Check for updates, as above.
Bluetooth and nearby devicesConnecting a Bluetooth gamepad. On Android 10 and 11 this permission is called Location, though SNESForge never reads where you are.
VibrationThe optional gentle vibration when you press a touch button.
NotificationsA notice while a game is running in the background.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove any game from its details page, with or without its saves, and uninstalling SNESForge deletes everything the app has kept. Files in the folders you scanned are never changed or deleted.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Children

SNESForge collects nothing from anyone. Younger players should ask a parent before adding games or switching on online box art.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
